02 CORE MECHANICS 課本 P.44 / 動名詞本質與拼字規則

核心定義：動詞變身名詞的兩大角色

課本開篇明確指出：We use the -ing form (infinitive + -ing) as a noun! 原本表示動作的動詞（例如 travel、swim、read），只要加上 -ing，就不再當動詞使用，而是變身為代表「一項活動或概念」的名詞。

角色一：主詞 (Subject) 必考單數規則

Travelling by aeroplane is wonderful.

搭乘飛機旅行是一件美好的事。這裡的 Travelling by aeroplane 是整個句子的主詞。

友寧必背考點：動名詞當主詞時，視為「單數第三人稱」，後面的 Be 動詞必須用 is 或 was，絕對不能用 are！
角色二：受詞 (Object)

I love travelling by aeroplane.

主詞是 I，一般動詞是 love，而 travelling 是接收喜愛動作的受詞。

  • She enjoys playing the piano.（她享受彈鋼琴。）
  • We finished doing our homework.（我們寫完功課了。）

動名詞變身公式與三寶典實驗室

結果： writing — 規則 2：字尾是不發音的 e，去 e 加 -ing（切勿寫成 writeing）。

03 FIVE TRIGGERS 課本 P.44 / 五大必加 -ing 時機與特殊片語

動名詞五大使用時機與介系詞鐵律

課本 P.44 歸納出五大必定或常接動名詞的情境。除了特定動詞與慣用語之外，最容易失分的「介系詞後面必加 -ing」與「look forward to」陷阱也收錄於此。

時機 01 Preference

表達「喜好與偏好」動詞

adore love like prefer enjoy dislike hate

課本例句：She hates waking up in the morning.（她討厭早上醒來。）

注意 wake 去 e 加 -ing。此類動詞中 like、love、hate、prefer 亦可接 to V（見單元四）。

時機 02 Beginning / End

表達「開始與結束」動詞

begin start continue finish (只接 V-ing) stop (語意不同)

課本例句：I started doing my homework at 6:00 p.m.
陷阱提醒：finish 後面只能接 V-ing（如 finished painting）。

時機 03 Specific Verbs

九大特定動詞天王團

avoid forgive imagine remember practise risk suggest mind keep
通關口訣：「建議 (suggest) 練習 (practise) 想像 (imagine)，避免 (avoid) 介意 (mind) 冒險 (risk)，保持 (keep) 原諒 (forgive) 與記憶 (remember)。」

例：My daughter practises driving a car once a week. / Sam suggested going to the match.

時機 04 Expressions

八大必背特殊片語與表達

  • can't help + V-ing（忍不住）：I can't help eating chocolate.
  • can't stand + V-ing（受不了）：can't stand washing the dishes.
  • It's no good / no use + V-ing（無益 / 沒用）：It's no use buying tickets.
  • It's worth + V-ing（值得）：It's worth visiting it.
  • How / What about + V-ing?（……怎麼樣？）：How about ordering a pizza?
大考第一魔王：look forward to + V-ing！
這裡的 to 是「介系詞」而非不定詞，必須接 V-ing：I am looking forward to seeing you soon.
時機 05 Preposition Rule

黃金鐵律：介系詞 (Preposition) 後面必加 -ing！

介系詞的天生使命是連接名詞。當動詞想放在介系詞（before, after, of, about, for, in, at, without）後面時，必須穿上 -ing 外套變身為動名詞。

介系詞 / 片語 中文意義 課本與習題經典例句
before / after + V-ing 在……之前 / 之後 I'd like to meet her before leaving the country.
be afraid of + V-ing 害怕…… I'm afraid of making her sad.
about / without + V-ing 關於…… / 沒有…… He talked about selling his car. / It's no good without knowing how to dance.
thank sb for + V-ing 感謝某人做…… Thank you for helping me with housework.
be good at + V-ing 擅長…… Chris is good at writing poems.
succeed in / interested in + V-ing 成功於…… / 對……有興趣 The girl succeeded in getting the job. / Diana is interested in learning the guitar.

04 SEMANTIC DUEL 課本 P.44 NOTE / 語意相同 vs 截然不同

觀念大對決：to V 與 -ing 語意比較

有些動詞後面接 to V 或 V-ing 意思完全一樣；但遇到「大魔王」stop 和 remember 時，選錯形式整句話的意思就會南轅北轍！

和平組：意思完全相同

七大隨和天王（零差別）

課本例句：They hate watching cartoons. = They hate to watch cartoons.

分類 動詞 對照範例
喜好組 (4) like, love, hate, prefer I like swimming. = I like to swim.
始末組 (3) begin, start, continue It began raining. = It began to rain.
魔王組：意思截然不同

stop & remember 語意分岔切換器

stop + V-ing（停止正在做的事） 當下動作畫句點

I stopped swimming. ➔ I did not swim anymore.

中文：我停止游泳了。（原本正在游，現在決定不游了。）課本實戰：The baby stopped crying.（小寶寶停止哭泣。）

ACT A 雙人訪談 5 大句型實戰 (課本 P.45) 5 題示範造句
1. What do you like doing in your free time? (like + V-ing)
示範造句：I like playing basketball and reading comic books in my free time.
2. Where do you enjoy going during your holidays? (enjoy + V-ing)
示範造句：I enjoy going to the seaside and swimming during my holidays.
3. What are you good at doing? (be good at + V-ing)
示範造句：I am good at solving difficult math problems and drawing.
4. What are you looking forward to doing next week? (look forward to + V-ing)
示範造句：I am looking forward to visiting the museum with my friends.
5. What can't you stand doing? (can't stand + V-ing)
示範造句：I can't stand washing greasy dishes after dinner.
ACT B 句型轉換挑戰 1–7 題 (課本 P.45) 7 題完整改寫
1. (示範) Why don't we order a pizza?
How about ordering a pizza? — Why don't we + 原形V ＝ How about + V-ing。
2. I laugh every time I see that film. I can't control myself.
I can't help laughing every time I see that film. — can't control myself ＝ can't help + V-ing（忍不住）。
3. Don't buy any tickets for the concert. We won't be able to go.
It's no use buying tickets for the concert. — 白費心力 ＝ It's no use + V-ing。
4. It was kind of you to help me with the housework. Thank you.
Thank you for helping me with the housework. — 介系詞 for + V-ing。
5. Chris writes poems. He's good at it.
Chris is good at writing poems. — at + V-ing，write 去 e 加 -ing。
6. My brother exercises all the time. He never stops.
My brother keeps exercising all the time. — never stops ＝ keep + V-ing，exercise 去 e 加 -ing。
7. Wendy must do her homework. It's 8:00 p.m.. She must start.
Wendy must start doing her homework. — start 接 doing（亦可接 to do）。
ACT C 看圖對話填空 1–6 題 (課本 P.46) 6 題情境轉述
1. (示範) "Let's go to the football match." ➔ Sam suggested...
Sam suggested going to the football match. — suggest 後必接 V-ing。
2. "I don't like washing the dishes. I hate it." ➔ The boys can't stand...
The boys can't stand washing the dishes. — can't stand + V-ing。
3. "Did you get the job?" "Yes, I did." ➔ The girl succeeded in...
The girl succeeded in getting the job. — in + V-ing，get 重複 t 加 -ing。
4. "Diana, would you like to learn how to play the guitar?" ➔ Diana is interested in...
Diana is interested in learning how to play the guitar. — be interested in + V-ing。
5. "I'm too busy at the moment to answer your question." ➔ The man avoided...
The man avoided answering the question (or her question). — avoid + V-ing。
6. "Hooray! I'm going on holiday tomorrow!" ➔ Maria and Nick are looking forward to...
Maria and Nick are looking forward to going on holiday. — look forward to + V-ing。
ACT D stop 與 remember 實戰填空 1–5 題 (課本 P.47) 5 題語意判斷
1. The baby stopped ______ (cry) when he saw his parents.
crying — 原本正在哭，停止哭泣動作。
2. Kelly remembered ______ (take) the books back to the library last Tuesday. She didn't forget.
to take — 提示 "She didn't forget" 代表記得去執行還書任務。
3. I remember ______ (play) chess with my cousin when I was younger.
playing — 小時候（過去回憶）曾做過的事。
4. On my way home from work, I stopped ______ (buy) some bread at the baker's.
to buy — 回家途中停下腳步，目的是去買麵包。
5. Dad: Did you remember ______ (phone) aunt Kelly? / Well, stop ______ (watch) TV and call her now.
1. to phone（記得去完成通話任務）；2. watching（命令停止當下正在看的電視）。

ACT F 提示字造句挑戰 1–6 題 (課本 P.48) 留意時態與使役動詞
1. (示範) Sam / refuse / wear / a hat / yesterday
Sam refused to wear a hat yesterday. — refuse + to V；yesterday 用過去式 refused。
2. The boy / finish / paint / the wardrobe / yesterday
The boy finished painting the wardrobe yesterday. — finish 必接 V-ing；yesterday 用過去式 finished。
3. Mrs Brown / make / the children / tidy up / their room / every Saturday
Mrs Brown makes the children tidy up their room every Saturday. — 使役動詞 make + 原形V；every Saturday 主詞單數用 makes。
4. Jill / be interested in / explore / caves
Jill is interested in exploring caves. — in + V-ing；explore 去 e 加 -ing。
5. Tony / practise / play / the drums / every day
Tony practises playing the drums every day. — practise + V-ing；主詞單數用 practises (美式 practices)。
6. They / be surprised / see / a monkey / on a busy street / yesterday
They were surprised to see a monkey on a busy street yesterday. — be surprised + to V 表情緒原因；yesterday 配 were。
ACT G 綜合選擇題 1–7 題 (課本 P.48) 7 題考點全解析
1. (示範) I don't mind ___ this film for a second time.
(b) watching — mind 後必接 V-ing。
2. ___ Japanese is difficult.
(c) Learning — 句首作主詞須用動名詞（To learning 語法錯誤，Learn 原形不可作主詞）。
3. It's good for you ___ a glass of milk every day.
(c) to drink — 經典虛主詞句型 It is + adj. + for sb + to V。
4. The Art Gallery is fantastic. It's worth ___ it.
(a) visiting — be worth + V-ing（值得做某事）。
5. My sister is thinking of ___ a dog.
(c) buying — 介系詞 of + V-ing。
6. ___ to parties is great fun!
(b) Going — 動名詞放句首作主詞。
7. Peter went to the library ___ a book on Egyptian History.
